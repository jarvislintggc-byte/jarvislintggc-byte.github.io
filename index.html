      Class

       The story plays automatically. Change class at the upper left and language at the upper right. Open Settings for speed and music. Play: use the left and right arrows to move. Space / W / ↑ jumps; press Jump again in midair to double jump. All classes can do this from Beginner. Hold ↑ / ↓ near ropes and ladders to climb, and press Space to jump off. Press ↓ + Space to drop through platforms. Hold ↓ on the ground to crouch. A attacks after dialogue ends and automatically uses your newest skill. You can move and jump during dialogue. If HP reaches zero, wait for the tombstone to land, then choose Revive Here or press Enter. After defeating a monster, keep moving while your three pets retrieve equipment across platforms. Press E beside an open portal. Enter advances dialogue. On touchscreens, tap the monster to attack or the blue portal to enter.
